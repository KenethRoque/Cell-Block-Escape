❓ HOW TO PLAY

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Find your key, avoid the guards and reach the prison exit.

👤 PLAYER 1

W A S D = Move

E = Hide / Leave Locker

👥 PLAYER 2

Arrow Keys = Move

Enter = Hide / Leave Locker

🔑 KEYS

You must collect your key before your exit opens.

In 2 Player mode each player has their own key.

🗄 HIDING

Walk onto a locker and press your hide button.

Guards cannot see you while you are hidden.

👮 GUARDS

🏁 2 PLAYER RACE

A random map is selected from 20 multiplayer maps.

🎮 ABOUT GAME

CELL BLOCK ESCAPE

🔒 THE GAME

Cell Block Escape is a top-down 2D prison escape game.

Explore prison mazes, avoid guards, hide in lockers, collect keys and escape.

👤 1 PLAYER

Escape through a 20-level campaign.

Levels 1–9 take place in the Old Prison.

Levels 10–20 take place in Maximum Security.

👥 2 PLAYER

Two prisoners race on the same keyboard.

One of 20 multiplayer maps is selected randomly for every match.

Each player has their own key and exit.

🏆 FIRST PLAYER OUT WINS!

👮 STEALTH

Guards patrol the prison, investigate the last place they saw a prisoner and chase visible prisoners.
