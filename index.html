❓ HOW TO PLAY

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Escape all 20 prison levels.

Find the 🔑 key before reaching the 🚪 exit gate.

🎮 CONTROLS

W A S D Move prisoner
↑ ↓ ← → Move prisoner
E Hide / leave locker
R Restart level
ENTER Start / restart game

🗄️ HIDING

Walk onto a locker and press E. Guards cannot see or catch you while you are hidden.

Press E again to leave.

👮 GUARDS

Guards patrol the prison.

! means the guard sees you.

? means the guard is searching for you.

🔑 KEY & 🚪 EXIT

The red exit is locked.

Collect the key and the exit becomes green.

❤️ LIVES

You have 3 lives. Getting caught removes one.

⏱ SCORE

Complete levels and keep your lives to earn more points.

Finish all 20 levels quickly for a time bonus.

🗺 LEVELS

Complete a level to unlock the next level.

Levels 10–20 take place in Maximum Security.
